# The $200/Day Prompt Chain (High-Ticket Systems Edition)
## 4-Step Socratic Progression for Technical Product Owners & Systems Architects

> "One prompt gives you a surface-level answer. Four prompts in sequence let Claude build a complete picture of who you are, what you should sell, how someone else is already doing it, and what is actually stopping you. Each answer feeds the next." — Maverick Maltin

---

## 1. Ground Rules for the Chain

1. **Single Continuous Session:** Never open a new chat between prompts. Context accumulation is the core mechanism.
2. **Context Pre-Loading:** Feed exact operational parameters before firing Prompt 1.
3. **Strict Constraint Enforcement:** Force the model to deliver **one offer and one channel**, eliminating generic laundry lists.

---

## 2. Context Injection Block (Tailored for Ariya Sarrafzadeh)

Copy and paste this initial block before triggering the chain:

```text
Here is my operational context before we begin:
- Name: Ariya Sarrafzadeh
- Core Role: Technical Product Owner / Solutions Architect / Senior Technical Product Manager
- Domain: Mega-apps, national-scale payment rails, distributed financial ledgers, transactional resilience, high-throughput systems (handling tens of millions of accounts and zero-discrepancy reconciliation).
- Target Markets: Dubai / UAE high-growth proptech/fintech ventures and global distributed infrastructure companies.
- Strategic Assets: Proven ability to eliminate 70% of alert noise, audit payment gateways, enforce settlement state invariants, and bridge business requirements with hardened engineering architecture.
- Available Capacity: 10-15 hours/week for fractional advisory or high-impact technical product leadership audits.
- Capital Budget: $0-$100 (pure skill, organic authority, and direct peer outreach).
- Hard Constraint: Zero corporate fluff, no generic "AI automation agency" claims, authentic systems-builder positioning only.
```

---

## 3. The 4 Core Prompts

### Prompt 1: The High-Signal Offer & Single Channel
```text
Based on everything you know about my background in national payment rails, transactional state boundaries, and technical product leadership, what is the single fastest, highest-conviction advisory or fractional offer I can take to market this week to generate an immediate $1,000–$2,000/week (or $200+/day)?
Limit your answer to ONE specific offer and ONE channel only.
No generic lists, no "become an AI consultant." Focus on an acute operational headache that enterprise fintech, proptech, or mega-app founders in Dubai/international markets are actively bleeding money on.
```

---

### Prompt 2: Reverse-Engineer the Market Blueprint
```text
Who is one real operator, advisory firm, or technical product leader already successfully selling this exact type of high-leverage architectural audit or fractional product leadership?
Give me their profile or business model and reverse-engineer their specific operational mechanics:
1. How do they frame the offer so it feels indispensable rather than discretionary?
2. What does their client acquisition funnel look like on LinkedIn?
3. What is their exact delivery artifact (e.g. 5-day state boundary audit, incident SOP playbook, ledger reconciliation blueprint)?
```

---

### Prompt 3: The 7-Day Zero-Overhead Action Plan
```text
Pretend you are that technical advisory operator. Build me an exact day-by-day 7-day action plan that requires under $100 to execute and secures my first paid client or advisory engagement.
Specify:
- What exact task I do each morning and afternoon.
- The exact LinkedIn direct-outreach message formula to send to CTOs, CPOs, and Founders.
- The exact qualification criteria to avoid time-wasters.
- The 1-page scope of work (SOW) template for closing the deal.
```

---

### Prompt 4: Socratic Bottleneck & 48-Hour Proving Run
```text
Act as my elite executive business coach. Ask me questions one at a time to uncover the single hesitation or operational friction that might prevent me from executing this outreach today.
Once I answer, reframe the constraint and prescribe an immediate 48-hour proving experiment that requires no new software, puts my positioning in front of 10 targeted decision-makers, and collects live market validation.
```

---

## 4. The Scale-Up Prompts (Post-Validation)

Once the first engagement is secured, run these scaling prompts in the same session:

### Scale Prompt 1: Price Anchoring & Retainer Conversion
```text
I have delivered the initial architectural audit for [Client]. How do I transition them into an ongoing $3,500/month fractional Technical Product Owner advisory retainer without sounding pushy? Give me the exact script and meeting agenda.
```

### Scale Prompt 2: Productized Diagnostic Asset
```text
Turn this manual architectural audit into a standardized, repeatable 10-point Platform Resilience Diagnostic that my LinkedIn carousel engine can showcase to attract inbound enterprise leads.
```
